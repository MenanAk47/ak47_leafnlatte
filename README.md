**The ultimate weed dispensary and cafe bar experience for FiveM servers.** Leaf & Latte delivers an all-in-one business ecosystem featuring multi-stage plant cultivation, crafting workstations, a full tablet dispensary shop, an interactive hookah lounge, and a cocktail bar. Built with performance and immersion in mind, it utilizes `ak47_lib` to guarantee turnkey compatibility across ESX, QBCore, and Qbox frameworks.

## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 🌿 Interactive Weed Cultivation & Farming

* **7-Stage Growth Lifecycle:** Plants dynamically transition through 7 distinct 3D visual models from seed in pot to full bloom.
* **Active Plant Care:** Crops occasionally require specialized maintenance (fertilizer or pesticide spray); growth pauses until attended to by a gardener.
* **Progressive Harvesting:** Harvest fully matured crops with synced animations to collect raw cannabis leaves for dispensary production.
* **Farming Supply Depots:** Integrated supplier points for employees to purchase seeds, pots, fertilizer, and spray bottles using society funds.

### 🧪 Workstation Crafting & Infused Products

* **Tablet Workstation UI:** Sleek modern interface for processing harvested cannabis into retail-ready merchandise.
* **30+ Flavored Backwoods & Joints:** Craft high-end strains including Glacatti, Hary Payton, Pure Runs, Snowberry Gelato, Boss Blend, and more.
* **Infused Edibles Creation:** Bake THC-infused edibles including Kush Krisps, Herbal Delight Gummies, Blaze Bites, Chronic Crunch Cookies, and HighTime Brownies.
* **Stress & Stat Relief:** Consuming edibles restores hunger, thirst, and directly relieves player stress levels.

### 🏪 Dispensary Storefront & Management

* **Modern Customer Tablet:** Category browsing (Flavours, Backwoods, Joints, Vape, Utilities, Edibles), real-time search, and multi-item shopping cart.
* **Flexible Payments:** Supports both cash and debit/credit card payments at customer checkout.
* **Employee Management Console:** Authorized staff can deposit inventory, withdraw stock, and adjust unit sale prices on the fly.
* **Society Fund Integration:** All shop revenue flows straight into the business society account to fund restocks and staff pay.

### 💨 Hookah Lounge & Synced Cocktail Bar

* **Dynamic Shisha Hose Physics:** Features 3D rope/hose physics connecting the player's pipe to the bong, complete with spatial 3D audio and synced mouth smoke particle FX.
* **Hookah Session & Refills:** Puffs per session tracking with on-table refill mechanics requiring water, weed leaves, and society balance.
* **Interactive Drink Stations:** Pour Cocktails, Champagne, Daiquiris, Wine, Whisky, Tequila Shots, Mojitos, and signature Leaf & Latte Coffee.
* **Bartender Tip Zone:** Dedicated tipping zone where customers can reward on-duty bartenders with custom hand-to-hand money exchange animations.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Persistent Data & Auto-Stocking:** Plant growth, drink inventory, and shop stocks persist across server restarts and txAdmin shutdowns via JSON datastores, with optional automated restocking timers.

## ⚙️ How To Install:

* Download ak47_leafnlatte from your cfx portal.
* Add the script in your resources folder.
* Add items based on your framework instruction.
* Add item images into inventory.
* Start the script in your server.cfg below framework files.
* Make sure you have all dependencies.
* Restart the server.
